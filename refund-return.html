Refund & Return Policy

This policy explains when customers may request a return, exchange, refund or cancellation for products purchased from Ayodeji Fashion Hubs.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our Commitment

We want every customer to receive the product they ordered in good condition and as described on our website.

If there is a problem with your order, contact us as soon as possible so we can review the issue and determine the appropriate solution.

2. When You May Request a Return

A return request may be considered where an item:

  • Is defective or damaged when received
  • Is materially different from the product ordered
  • Is the wrong product or size supplied by us
  • Has another significant issue covered by applicable consumer law

Return requests are reviewed based on the individual circumstances of the order.

3. Reporting a Problem

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact us as soon as reasonably possible.

Please provide:

  • Your order reference
  • Your name and phone number
  • A description of the problem
  • Clear photographs or videos where relevant

This information helps us investigate the issue and determine the appropriate resolution.

4. Condition of Returned Products

Where a return is approved, the product should generally be returned in a condition that allows us to inspect it.

Depending on the circumstances, we may consider the product's packaging, tags, accessories and other items supplied with the order.

This requirement does not remove any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

5. Wrong Product or Size

If we send you a product that is different from the product you ordered, contact us before using or altering the product.

Where the error is confirmed to be ours, we may arrange an appropriate replacement, exchange or refund depending on availability and the circumstances of the order.

6. Damaged Products

If your order arrives visibly damaged, document the condition of the package and product where possible and contact us promptly.

We may request photographs, videos or other information needed to investigate the issue with the delivery provider or other relevant party.

7. Change-of-Mind Returns

A return based only on a change of mind is not automatically guaranteed.

Where we voluntarily accept a change-of-mind return, the applicable conditions, product condition, delivery costs and any other agreed terms will be communicated before the return is completed.

This does not affect any mandatory consumer rights provided by applicable law.

8. Refunds

Where a refund is approved, the refund amount will depend on the circumstances of the order and the applicable consumer-protection requirements.

Refunds may be issued through an appropriate payment method or another method agreed with the customer, subject to verification.

We may request information necessary to verify the original transaction before processing a refund.

9. Deposit Payments

If an order was placed using the 60% deposit option, any approved refund will be assessed based on the reason for the refund, the status of the order and applicable law.

A deposit does not automatically become non-refundable merely because it was described as a deposit.

Where a refund is legally or contractually due, the applicable refundable amount will be communicated to the customer.

10. Orders That Have Not Yet Been Dispatched

If you request cancellation before an order has been dispatched, contact us immediately.

We will review the cancellation request and inform you whether cancellation is possible and whether a refund is applicable.

11. Orders Already Dispatched

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

If there is a legitimate problem with the product, contact us after receiving it and we will review the matter under this policy and applicable consumer rights.

12. Cash on Delivery Orders

For Cash on Delivery orders, customers should inspect the order as reasonably possible upon delivery.

If there is a clear issue with the order, contact us promptly with the order reference and relevant evidence.

13. Return Delivery Costs

Responsibility for return delivery costs depends on the reason for the return.

Where the return is caused by an error, defect or other circumstance for which Ayodeji Fashion Hubs is responsible, we will communicate the applicable return arrangements.

For other types of returns, the customer may be responsible for applicable return or delivery costs, where legally permitted and where this has been communicated to the customer.

14. Exchanges

Where an exchange is approved, replacement depends on product and size availability.

If the requested replacement is unavailable, we may discuss another appropriate solution with the customer.

15. Items That Have Been Used or Altered

Customers should not intentionally damage, modify or materially alter a product before contacting us about a return.

Where damage or alteration is caused after delivery by the customer, it may affect the available return or refund options, subject to applicable law.

16. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed within a reasonable period after the required verification and return process has been completed.

The time for the money to appear in the customer's account may also depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

17. Fraudulent or Abusive Claims

We may investigate suspicious, fraudulent or inconsistent return and refund requests.

Providing false information or attempting to abuse the return or refund process may result in the request being rejected and may lead to appropriate action where necessary.

18. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove, restrict or replace any consumer rights that are mandatory under applicable Nigerian law.

Where a legal right gives a customer a remedy that differs from this policy, the applicable legal requirement will apply.

19. How to Contact Us

To request a return, exchange, refund or cancellation, contact Ayodeji Fashion Hubs with your order reference and a clear explanation of the issue.

Please keep your order reference available when contacting us so that we can locate your order.

20. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund & Return Policy when our business practices, services or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
