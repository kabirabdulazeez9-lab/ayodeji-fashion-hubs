Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Ayodeji Fashion Hubs collects, uses, stores and protects your information when you use our website and services.

Last updated: September 2026

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Ayodeji Fashion Hubs and explains how information is handled when you browse our website, create an account, place an order, make a payment, contact us or use our order-tracking services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Delivery city and state
  • Order and purchase information
  • Products, sizes and quantities ordered
  • Payment-related information
  • Information you provide when contacting us
  • Account information required to provide our services

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your customer account
  • Process and fulfil your orders
  • Arrange delivery of your purchases
  • Confirm payments and payment status
  • Provide order tracking
  • Contact you about your order
  • Send important order-status notifications
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our website and services
  • Prevent fraud, abuse or unauthorized activity
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations

4. Payments

Ayodeji Fashion Hubs may provide payment options including OPay bank transfer and Cash on Delivery, where available.

We do not request or store your bank PIN, ATM PIN, card PIN or banking password through our website. You should never send such confidential information to us through chat, email or any other communication channel.

Where a third-party payment or financial service is used, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

5. Customer Accounts

If you create an account, we use the information associated with your account to provide features such as profile management, checkout assistance and access to your customer information.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and should notify us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

6. Order Tracking

Our order-tracking service may require your order reference and customer phone number to locate and display information about your order.

Please do not share your order information with people you do not trust.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information when reasonably necessary to provide our services, including with:

  • Delivery or logistics providers
  • Payment and financial service providers
  • Technology and hosting service providers
  • Communication or notification service providers
  • Government authorities where legally required

We only intend to share information necessary for the relevant service or legal requirement.

8. Data Storage and Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect customer information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use browser technologies such as cookies or local storage to support functionality, remember certain settings and maintain shopping-cart information.

Disabling certain browser storage or cookies may affect some website functionality.

10. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or comply with legal obligations.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the ability to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain information.

If you have a privacy-related request, contact Ayodeji Fashion Hubs using the contact information provided on our website.

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for customers who are able to enter into transactions under applicable law. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

13. Third-Party Services

Our website may rely on third-party services for hosting, authentication, databases, payments, communications or other functionality.

Those providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Ayodeji Fashion Hubs through the contact details provided on our website.
